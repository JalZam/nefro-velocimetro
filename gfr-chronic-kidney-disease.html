What the Kidney Speedometer is

Velocímetro Renal, Spanish for “Kidney Speedometer” (velocimetrorenal.com), is a free web tool for the physician to explain to the patient, during the visit, their kidney function and their risk of progression of chronic kidney disease. The screen speaks to the patient with a simple comparison: just as a car has a speedometer, the kidneys have their own “speed”, the glomerular filtration rate. The higher it is, the better the kidney works. It is available in Spanish and in English.

The tool does five things:

  • It shows GFR as the needle of a speedometer over the KDIGO stages G1 to G5.
  • It calculates GFR with the CKD-EPI 2021 formula (creatinine, or creatinine and cystatin C), without the race variable.
  • It classifies albuminuria as A1, A2 or A3 according to the urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio.
  • It places the patient on the KDIGO risk grid (GFR × albuminuria), with the level of risk and the yearly check-up frequency for their cell.
  • It produces a handout for the patient, to print or save as a PDF.

What GFR is

The glomerular filtration rate (GFR; eGFR when it is estimated; TFG in Spanish) is the measure of how well the kidneys filter. It is expressed in mL/min/1.73 m². On the speedometer, the needle moves to the right the better the kidney filters; the scale goes from 0 to 120.

The outlook for the kidney does not depend on GFR alone: albuminuria weighs just as much. That is why the tool asks for both values and combines them in the risk grid.

How it is calculated: CKD-EPI 2021

If the GFR is not available, the tool estimates it with the CKD-EPI 2021 equations (Inker et al., N Engl J Med 2021), which do not use the race variable. There are two versions.

Creatinine only

GFR = 142 × min(Cr/κ, 1)α × max(Cr/κ, 1)−1.200 × 0.9938age × 1.012 (if female)

  • Cr: serum creatinine in mg/dL. Age in years.
  • κ: 0.7 for women and 0.9 for men.
  • α: −0.241 for women and −0.302 for men.

Creatinine and cystatin C

GFR = 135 × min(Cr/κ, 1)α × max(Cr/κ, 1)−0.544 × min(Cys/0.8, 1)−0.323 × max(Cys/0.8, 1)−0.778 × 0.9961age × 0.963 (if female)

  • Cys: serum cystatin C in mg/L.
  • κ: 0.7 for women and 0.9 for men.
  • α: −0.219 for women and −0.144 for men.

Examples

  • A 55-year-old man with a creatinine of 1.2 mg/dL (creatinine only): GFR of 71.4 mL/min/1.73 m², stage G2.
  • A 60-year-old woman with a creatinine of 1 mg/dL and a cystatin C of 1.1 mg/L: GFR of 66.1 mL/min/1.73 m², stage G2.

The calculator rounds the result to one decimal place and accepts ages from 18 years.

G1 and G2 with albuminuria A1 are only considered chronic kidney disease if another marker of kidney damage is present.

Albuminuria categories (A1 to A3)

Albuminuria is measured as the albumin-to-creatinine ratio in a urine sample (uACR), in mg/g.

CategoryuACRNameWhat it means
A1< 30 mg/gNormal to mildly increasedNo significant increase of protein in the urine.
A230–300 mg/gModerately increasedModerate increase in albumin. Indicates early kidney damage.
A3> 300 mg/gSeverely increasedSevere proteinuria. High risk of kidney disease progression.

The same grid, in text:

  • G1 (GFR ≥ 90): with A1, low risk (1 check-up per year); with A2, moderate risk (1 check-up per year); with A3, high risk (2 check-ups per year).
  • G2 (GFR 60 – 89): with A1, low risk (1 check-up per year); with A2, moderate risk (1 check-up per year); with A3, high risk (2 check-ups per year).
  • G3a (GFR 45 – 59): with A1, moderate risk (1 check-up per year); with A2, high risk (2 check-ups per year); with A3, very high risk (3 check-ups per year).
  • G3b (GFR 30 – 44): with A1, high risk (2 check-ups per year); with A2, very high risk (3 check-ups per year); with A3, very high risk (3 check-ups per year).
  • G4 (GFR 15 – 29): with A1, very high risk (3 check-ups per year); with A2, very high risk (3 check-ups per year); with A3, very high risk (4+ check-ups per year).
  • G5 (GFR < 15): with A1, very high risk (4+ check-ups per year); with A2, very high risk (4+ check-ups per year); with A3, very high risk (4+ check-ups per year).

“4+” means four or more check-ups per year.

How to use it during the visit

  1. If you already have the patient's GFR and albuminuria (uACR), enter them directly.
  2. If you need to calculate the GFR, open “Calculate GFR with CKD-EPI 2021”, enter creatinine, age and sex (optionally cystatin C) and press “Calculate”.
  3. The speedometer shows the patient where their kidney function stands: the needle moves to the right the better the kidney filters.
  4. With GFR and albuminuria, the KDIGO grid highlights the patient's cell and their risk of progression; use it to explain why albuminuria weighs as much as GFR.
  5. Finally, “Print patient handout” produces a black-and-white summary to hand over, with optional space for the patient's name and the physician's.

Privacy

All the calculation happens in the browser. GFR, albuminuria, creatinine, cystatin C, age and sex are not sent to any server, are not stored and disappear when the page is closed or reloaded. There are no cookies and no advertising. The details are in the “Data use policy” at the foot of the calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is Velocímetro Renal (the Kidney Speedometer)?

It is a free web tool for the physician to explain to the patient, during the visit, how their kidney function is doing. It shows the glomerular filtration rate (GFR) as the needle of a speedometer over the KDIGO stages G1 to G5, classifies albuminuria (A1 to A3) and places the patient on the KDIGO risk grid.

Which formula does it use to calculate GFR?

CKD-EPI 2021, with creatinine alone or with creatinine and cystatin C. These are the equations published by Inker and colleagues in the New England Journal of Medicine in 2021. They do not use the race variable.

What data are needed to calculate GFR?

Sex, age in years and serum creatinine in mg/dL. Optionally, cystatin C in mg/L. If the GFR is already known, it can be typed in directly without calculating it.

What are the stages of chronic kidney disease by GFR?

G1: GFR ≥ 90 mL/min/1.73 m² (normal or high); G2: GFR 60 – 89 mL/min/1.73 m² (mildly decreased); G3a: GFR 45 – 59 mL/min/1.73 m² (mildly to moderately decreased); G3b: GFR 30 – 44 mL/min/1.73 m² (moderately to severely decreased); G4: GFR 15 – 29 mL/min/1.73 m² (severely decreased); G5: GFR < 15 mL/min/1.73 m² (kidney failure). G1 and G2 with albuminuria A1 are only considered chronic kidney disease if another marker of kidney damage is present.

How is albuminuria classified?

A1: < 30 mg/g (normal to mildly increased); A2: 30–300 mg/g (moderately increased); A3: > 300 mg/g (severely increased). It is measured as the urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (uACR).

What is the KDIGO risk grid?

It is a table that crosses the GFR stage (G1 to G5) with the albuminuria category (A1 to A3). Each cell indicates the risk of progression (low, moderate, high or very high) and how many kidney function check-ups are recommended per year. It shows that albuminuria weighs as much as GFR in the outlook.

Does the Kidney Speedometer store patient data?

No. All the calculation happens in the browser. The values are not sent to any server, are not stored and disappear when the page is closed or reloaded. It uses no cookies and no advertising.

Is it meant for self-diagnosis?

No. It is a support tool for the consultation. Interpretation is the responsibility of the treating physician.

Which languages is it available in?

Spanish and English. Use the ES | EN switch in the header.

How much does it cost?

It is free and has no advertising.
